W A S D
Walk. Arrow keys walk and turn
Mouse
Look. Click the view to capture the mouse, Esc releases it
Drag
Look without capturing the mouse
Shift
Walk faster (a brisk jog)
V
Natural or wide field of view (the Wide view button)
E
Use what you look at: doors, wardrobes, drawers, boxes, windows (tilt), curtains, chairs and sofas (sit), beds (lie down), the bunk-bed ladder. E again to stand up
C
Crouch or stand up, for example to peek under the bunk bed
T
Tape measure out or away (the Tape button). Aim the crosshair at a point and click or press Q to set the start, again for the end. X clears the measurements
M
Show or hide the plan. Click the plan to jump to a spot
O
View from above. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and peel off floors with Show
G
Foam-dart blaster on or off (the Blaster button). It shows while you walk
F
Fire a dart (hold to keep firing)
R
Reload the 12-dart magazine from your spare darts. Walk over darts on the floor to pick them up

Stairs work like the real ones: walk into them to go up or down. The main stair links all three levels.
